About

The person behind Nuvela.

I'm Natasha, founder of Nuvela Creative Co. I help small businesses, makers and community organizations look professional and run more smoothly, through thoughtful branding, well-built websites and practical custom apps.

The name comes from nu, meaning new, and novela, meaning story. That's what I help businesses create: a fresh chapter, told well.

My work ranges from a nail artist's online shop and a scholarship pageant's fundraising sites to a crochet pattern app and an on-site estimating tool for a flooring company. Every project starts the same way: understanding how your business runs, who your customers are, and what story you want to tell.

You work directly with me from the first conversation through launch and beyond. I explain things in plain language, keep pricing clear, and set you up so you can make everyday updates yourself.

  • Custom work, never templates
  • Built for phones first
  • Readable and accessible
  • Support after launch
