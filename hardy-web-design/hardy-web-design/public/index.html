About me

The person behind Hardy.

I'm Natasha, the designer and developer behind Hardy Web Design. I build custom websites and web tools for small businesses, makers and community organizations that want to look professional online and make their day-to-day work easier.

My work ranges from a nail artist's online shop and a scholarship pageant's fundraising sites to a crochet pattern app and an on-site estimating tool for a flooring company. Each project starts the same way: understanding how your business runs, who your customers are, and what you need your website to do.

You work directly with me from the first conversation through launch and beyond. I explain things in plain language, keep pricing clear, and set your site up so you can make everyday updates yourself.

  • 🎨 Custom design, never cookie-cutter
  • 📱 Built for phones first
  • ♿ Readable and accessible for everyone
  • 🤝 Friendly support after launch
